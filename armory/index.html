Authorised use only. This collection includes dual-use security tooling. High-risk items such as credential-access utilities, C2 frameworks, tunnelling tools, wireless attack tooling, exploitation frameworks and invasive scanners are documented here for isolated training ranges, defensive detection engineering, or engagements with explicit written authorisation. The Armory deliberately does not provide operational intrusion recipes, stealth instructions, payload deployment steps or third-party targeting guidance.
